Why GeoCam?

You draw a zone around your home in the app. When you leave and cross that zone, Blink arms. When you come back and re-enter, Blink disarms. One setup, then it runs in the background—no more "did I turn the cameras on?"

Home is always protected

Zero daily effort

Stop opening the Blink app every time you leave or get home. GeoCam handles it so you can focus on your day.

Flexible for families

Adjust the zone size and sensitivity to fit your routine. With Pro, add family members so Blink arms only when everyone has left—perfect for households with different schedules.

How it works

You choose the area around your home (the "geofence"). When your phone leaves that area, GeoCam tells Blink to arm. When you come back inside the zone, it disarms. You can tweak the zone size and, with Pro, add other family phones so the system knows when everyone's away. It's built only for Blink—no extra hubs or hardware.

Your Blink data never leaves your device

GeoCam talks directly to Blink — no proxy, no middleman, no data on our servers.

Credentials on-device only

Your Blink username and password are stored locally on your phone and never transmitted to GeoCam servers.

Direct to Blink

All API calls go straight from your phone to Blink's servers. GeoCam acts as no relay or intermediary.

Location stays local

Your GPS coordinates are processed on-device to detect geofence transitions. Your location is never uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GeoCam?

GeoCam is a geofencing app for Blink home security cameras. It automatically arms your Blink cameras when you leave home and disarms them when you return, using your phone's location.

How does GeoCam work with Blink cameras?

You draw a zone around your home on a map inside the app. When your phone leaves that zone, GeoCam tells Blink to arm all cameras. When you re-enter the zone, it disarms them. Setup takes about two minutes.

Does GeoCam work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. GeoCam is available on both the Apple App Store (iOS 15+) and Google Play Store. The experience is the same on both platforms.

Can multiple family members use GeoCam?

Yes. With the GeoCam Pro plan, you can add family members so your Blink cameras only arm when everyone has left home. This prevents false arms when one person is still inside.

How much does GeoCam cost?

GeoCam costs €3.99 per month or €39.99 per year. The annual plan includes a 7-day free trial so you can try it risk-free before committing.

Is GeoCam safe and private?

Yes. GeoCam only uses your location to detect home-zone transitions. Your GPS coordinates are processed on-device and are never stored on our servers. Blink credentials are handled through secure OAuth authentication.
