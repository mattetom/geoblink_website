Description of the Service

GeoCam is a mobile application that adds geofencing capabilities to Blink Home Monitor smart security cameras. The Application uses Your device's location to automatically arm or disarm Your Blink camera systems based on Your proximity to a designated home area.

Key features include:

  • Automatic arm/disarm: Camera systems are armed when You leave Your geofence and disarmed when You return, without requiring manual interaction.

  • Geofence configuration: You can define a custom geofence location and radius via the in-app map.

  • Family Geofencing (Pro): Multiple household members can participate in geofencing so that cameras are only armed when all registered members have left home, and disarmed as soon as the first member returns.

  • Background operation: The Application monitors Your location in the background to detect geofence entry and exit events even when the Application is not in the foreground.
